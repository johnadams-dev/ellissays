The Process

How Ellis delivers your leads.

Simple, transparent, and always with homeowner consent at every step.

01
Homeowner completes the Ellis advisor flow

They enter their address, see their valuation panel, answer the trade-off sliders, and receive their recommendation.

02
Ellis asks if they want investor contact

Homeowners indicating urgency or selecting cash offer paths see a clear, plain-language opt-in: "Would you like to be contacted by local cash buyers before your home is listed publicly?"

03
You receive an alert within your window

Within 48–72 hours of opt-in (depending on your tier), you receive the lead notification with full property and seller profile.

04
You reach out directly

Premium subscribers receive a warm introduction. Basic and Professional subscribers receive contact information to reach out independently. The homeowner knows to expect you.
