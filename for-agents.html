Ellis for Agents

Qualified referrals.
No cold leads.

Ellis agent partners receive sellers who have already decided they want full-service representation — after going through an honest, data-driven process that showed them all their options.

What Makes an Ellis Referral Different

These sellers already know what they're getting into.

An Ellis referral arrives knowing the current market value of their home, having seen the honest data about what agent-assisted sales produce, and having actively chosen full-service representation over every alternative. That's a different conversation than a cold lead.

Pre-educated sellers

They've seen four independent valuations, understand the market, and know realistic pricing. You spend less time managing expectations and more time selling.

Committed to representation

Ellis referred them because their trade-off profile indicated full-service is the right choice. They didn't come to you through a cold portal ad — they came through a decision process.

Three agents, three appointments

Ellis presents three matched agents and schedules appointments directly. You're not competing with ten other agents — you're one of three, matched on your actual performance data.

Matched on your strengths

Ellis matches based on proven performance in the seller's price range, neighborhood, and property type — not proximity or ad spend. Good agents get more referrals.

No upfront cost to you

Ellis earns a referral fee at closing — a standard 25–35% of your listing commission. Nothing changes until the transaction closes. You owe nothing if it doesn't.

Pre-listing homeowners too

Sellers who complete the Ellis flow but aren't ready to commit immediately stay in the system. Ellis can refer them to you when the time comes — before they start Googling.

How It Works

Simple from your end.

01
You join the Ellis partner network

Submit your performance data — price ranges, neighborhoods, recent sales. Ellis uses this to match you to the right sellers.

02
A seller matches your profile

When a seller's trade-off profile indicates full-service is right and their property matches your strengths, Ellis includes you in their three-agent recommendation.

03
Ellis schedules the appointment

You receive a notification with the seller's full property profile and a confirmed appointment time. No chasing. No cold outreach.

04
You close the listing

Standard referral agreement. Ellis receives 25–35% of your listing commission at closing. Everything else is your standard business.
